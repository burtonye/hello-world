🍎 Fruit Box Game 🍎

Draw boxes around apples that sum to 10!

How to Play:

  • Players take turns drawing boxes around apples
  • Click and drag to select apples in a rectangular area
  • The sum of selected apples must equal exactly 10
  • Each player gets 1 point for a valid box
  • First to 10 points wins!
